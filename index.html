EarthResourceML

EarthResourceML is an XML-based data transfer standard for mineral occurrences, mines and mining activity. Information about EarthResourceML and EarthResourceML-Lite, including the current versions, documentation and previous versions, is now on the CGI website.

Go to EarthResourceML on cgi-iugs.org

The documentation, XML schemas and examples on this site stay at their existing addresses, such as earthresourceml.org/schemas/, earthresourceml.org/earthresourceml/ and earthresourceml.org/earthresourceml-lite/. Links to them, including schemaLocation references in XML documents, keep working.

EarthResourceML is maintained by the IUGS Commission for the Management and Application of Geoscience Information (CGI).
